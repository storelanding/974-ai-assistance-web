Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about Ai assistance.

What is Ai assistance?

Ai assistance is an AI assistant app for Android phones, available on Google Play. It brings AI chat, an AI writer, translation, text scanning, photo tools and creative generators together in one app.

What kind of content can the AI writer create?

Persuasive sales copy, engaging blog posts and social media captions, in a range of writing styles and tones. You keep full control over the final text, so it stays on-brand for your audience.

Can I edit photos with the app?

Yes. You can enhance photos, restore old photos, remove objects, apply AI filters, try on outfits and change hairstyles, then save or share the result.

Can it translate and read text from images?

Yes. AI Translate converts text between languages, and Scan Text extracts the words from a photo, for example a page of a book.

Is there a Premium subscription?

The app offers an optional Premium (PRO) subscription. It is purchased and managed through Google Play, and you can cancel it at any time in your Google Play subscriptions. See our Terms of Use.

Which devices are supported?

Ai assistance is an Android app. Download it from Google Play on your Android phone.
