Effective date: November 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VIPRO AI ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the Ai assistance mobile application for Android, distributed on Google Play (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

Third-party services

The App uses the following third-party services. Each service may collect and process information according to its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob: displays advertising in the App. Google Privacy Policy
  • Meta Audience Network: displays advertising in the App. Meta Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics: helps us understand how the App is used. Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Analytics: app usage analytics. Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Adjust: measures app installs and marketing performance. Adjust Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reporting, so we can find and fix errors. Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Firebase Performance Monitoring: measures app performance, such as loading times. Firebase Privacy and Security

Advertising

The App shows ads through Google AdMob and Meta Audience Network. These networks may use your device's advertising identifier (Advertising ID) to show ads, measure their performance and, where permitted, personalize them based on your interests.

You can limit personalized advertising at any time: on most Android devices, open Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) and choose to delete or reset your Advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization. Where required by law, the App asks for your consent before showing personalized ads.

Subscriptions

The App offers an optional Premium subscription. Payments are processed by Google Play; we do not receive or store your full payment card details. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in the Google Play Store app under Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.
